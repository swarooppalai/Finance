Introduction to AlphaEdge Financials

What this platform is, who it's for, and what you will find here.

AlphaEdge Financials is an exclusive, subscription-based research and advisory portal built for serious investors in Indian equity markets. It delivers institutional-grade research — including weekly option selling strategies, a curated 10-stock model portfolio, IPO analysis, and macroeconomic market updates — all through a clean, live-data web interface.

What AlphaEdge Provides

  • Weekly defined-risk option selling strategies on Nifty, Bank Nifty & Finnifty with exact strike levels, margins, risk, and targets

Accessing the Portal

How to open and view the AlphaEdge platform on your device.

💡
Best Experience
For the best experience, open the portal in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop or laptop. The site is also fully functional on tablets and mobile devices.
1

Locate the Portal File

You have received the portal as a folder containing three files: index.html, styles.css, and app.js. Keep all three files in the same folder — they must remain together.

2

Open in Browser

Double-click index.html — it will open automatically in your default browser. Alternatively, drag the file and drop it directly into a Chrome or Edge browser window.

3

Allow Internet Connection

Ensure you have an active internet connection. The portal loads live fonts and the Chart.js library from a CDN (Content Delivery Network) to render charts and graphs. Without internet, charts may not appear.

4

Wait for Full Load

Once the page loads, you will see the live market ticker scrolling at the top, the navigation bar, and the main dashboard. Live toast notifications (pop-up alerts) will appear in the bottom-right corner after a few seconds.

⚡ Option Selling Hub

Weekly defined-risk option strategies with full transparency.

Option Selling — AlphaEdge
Fig 4 — Option Selling Hub showing active strategy cards with full metrics, strike levels, and capital utilisation bars.
⚡
Defined-Risk Only — No Naked Options
AlphaEdge only publishes spread strategies (Bull Put Spreads, Iron Condors, Bear Call Spreads). These have a pre-defined, capped maximum loss — meaning your risk is known before entering the trade. Naked short options are never recommended.

Reading a Strategy Card

①

Strategy Name & Type

E.g. "Nifty Bull Put Spread — Weekly Defined Risk". The badge shows if it is Active (live), Pending (not yet entered), or Closed.

②

Strike Levels (Blue Box)

The cyan-highlighted box shows the exact option legs. Example: Sell 24,700 PE | Buy 24,500 PE. This is the trade to execute. Premium users see actual strikes; Basic users see it blurred.

③

Key Metrics Grid

Net Premium = Premium collected (income). Margin Required = Capital blocked by broker. Max Risk = Worst-case loss. Max Profit = Best case. Target Exit = Recommended profit booking level. Win Rate = Historical % of winning trades.

④

Capital Utilisation Bar

The progress bar at the card bottom shows what % of the ₹1,00,000 reference capital is being used as margin for this strategy. A utilisation of 72.5% means ₹72,500 of ₹1,00,000 is engaged.

⑤

This Week P&L

Shown in the bottom-right of each card. Green = profit, Red = loss. This is updated weekly after the options expiry cycle closes.

Understanding the Portfolio Table

  • Ticker — NSE/BSE symbol of the stock (e.g. RELIANCE, HDFCBANK)
  • Allocation % — What portion of the ₹1,00,000 portfolio is invested in this stock
  • Entry Price — The price at which the position was initiated (Premium subscribers only)

  • Target — Analyst price target over 12–18 months (Premium only)
  • Stop-Loss — Recommended exit level if the trade goes wrong (Premium only)
  • P&L % — Unrealized gain/loss from entry price. Green = profit, Red = loss
  • Verdict — BUY / HOLD / STRONG BUY badge. Guides ongoing action

Reading an IPO Card

Price Band The minimum and maximum IPO issue price set by the company. You apply at the upper end (cut-off price) for best allotment chances.
Issue Size Total amount the company is raising through the IPO (in crores). Larger issue = more shares available, but may also mean lower oversubscription.
QIB / NII / Retail Subscription multiples for each investor category. QIB = Qualified Institutional Buyers. NII = Non-Institutional Investors (HNI). Retail = Individual investors applying up to ₹2L. Higher multiples = strong demand.
GMP (Grey Market Premium) Unofficial expected listing premium. E.g. GMP of ₹88 on a ₹400 IPO = expected listing at ~₹488 (+22%). Available to Premium/Elite subscribers only. Not guaranteed.
Analyst Verdict "Subscribe (Listing Gains)" = Apply for short-term listing pop. "Apply (Long Term)" = Good business but may not list at a big premium. "Avoid" = Risk outweighs reward. "Watch" = Decision pending; monitor closer to open date.

📊 Analytics & Performance

Full historical performance data, returns ledger, and benchmark comparison.

Analytics — AlphaEdge
Fig 8 — Analytics section showing CAGR/IRR KPIs, benchmark comparison chart, monthly P&L bars, and the full returns history table.

Key Performance Metrics Explained

  • CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) — Annualised return if you had stayed invested since inception. 24.52% means ₹1,00,000 grows to ~₹1,24,520 in one year at this rate.
  • IRR (Internal Rate of Return) — Time-weighted return accounting for exact investment dates and cash flows. More precise than CAGR. Our IRR is 22.31%.
  • Win Rate — Percentage of option strategies that closed at a profit. 68.75% means 11 of 16 trades were winners.
  • Max Drawdown — Worst peak-to-trough loss in any single period. Our max drawdown was -6.21% in April 2026 — within acceptable risk bounds.
  • Alpha — Outperformance over the NIFTY 50 benchmark. Our +15.21% alpha means we beat the index by 15.21 percentage points since inception.
📋
Monthly Returns Table
The returns table shows capital deployed, net profit, profit percentage, CAGR, and IRR for every month since inception. Green values are profitable months. Red values are losing months. Use this to assess consistency over time, not just recent performance.

Glossary of Terms

Quick reference for financial and platform-specific terminology.

NIFTY 50India's benchmark stock market index comprising the 50 largest companies listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE).
BANK NIFTYNSE's banking sector index comprising the 12 most liquid Indian banking stocks. Widely used for option trading.
VIX (India VIX)Volatility Index — measures expected market volatility over next 30 days. Lower VIX (below 15) = calm market, good for option selling. Higher VIX = higher premiums but more risk.
Bull Put SpreadSell a lower-strike Put option, Buy an even lower-strike Put option. Profits if the market stays flat or rises. Loss is capped. Used in neutral-to-bullish markets.
Iron CondorCombination of a Bull Put Spread and Bear Call Spread. Profits if the market stays within a range. Used when expecting low volatility.
Bear Call SpreadSell a lower-strike Call, Buy a higher-strike Call. Profits if market stays flat or falls. Used in neutral-to-bearish markets.
Net PremiumThe total option premium received after buying the hedge leg. This is the maximum profit if the strategy expires at zero (fully worthless).
Margin RequiredAmount of capital your broker will block as collateral to hold the option position. Not an actual cost — just reserved funds.
CAGRCompound Annual Growth Rate — the annualised return assuming profits are compounded. Best metric for long-term strategy comparison.
IRRInternal Rate of Return — accounts for exact timing of capital deployment. More accurate than CAGR when capital is deployed gradually.
PCR (Put-Call Ratio)Ratio of Put option open interest to Call option open interest. PCR above 1.2 is considered bullish (more puts written = market expects upside).
Max PainThe strike price at which maximum option contracts (both calls and puts) expire worthless. Markets often gravitate toward max pain at expiry.
FII / DIIForeign Institutional Investors / Domestic Institutional Investors. FII net buying = bullish signal. FII selling = bearish pressure on Indian markets.
GMP (Grey Market Premium)Unofficial price at which IPO shares are trading in the unlisted / grey market before official listing. An informal indicator of listing demand.
Stop-Loss (SL)A pre-determined price level at which you exit a position to limit your loss. Always honour the stop-loss to protect capital.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from subscribers.

The charts are not loading / appearing blank. What should I do?
The charts are powered by Chart.js, which loads from the internet. Ensure you have an active internet connection and try refreshing the page (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R). If using a corporate network, check if CDN URLs are blocked by your firewall.
The content is blurred / locked even after logging in. Why?
Locked content is based on your subscription tier. Blurred option strikes require Premium or Elite access. The blurred 3rd strategy card (Finnifty Bear Call) requires Elite access. Use the role selector in the Login modal to apply the correct tier in the demo version.
How often are the option strategies updated?
Option strategies are published fresh every week — typically on Monday or Tuesday morning before 10:00 AM. Updates (target achieved, exit signals) are published intraday as required and pushed via the Updates feed and Telegram/WhatsApp.
How often is the model portfolio (AD Active 10) rebalanced?
The portfolio is rebalanced as needed — typically once a month or when a fundamental or technical trigger justifies a change. Every rebalance is documented with full rationale in the Rebalance Log on the Dashboard and in the Updates feed.
Are the performance figures audited?
All performance figures are self-reported and based on actual published strategies. Every trade entry and exit is published in real-time via the platform and Telegram. SEBI requires research analysts to maintain a transparent track record, which we adhere to fully.
Can I follow these strategies with any amount of capital?
The model uses ₹1,00,000 as the reference capital for option strategies. In practice, each weekly strategy requires approximately ₹70,000–₹1,00,000 in margin depending on the strategy. For the equity portfolio (AD Active 10), you can scale proportionally to your capital.
What is the cancellation policy?
You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account page. Cancellation stops future billing but does not provide a refund for the current billing period (except within the 7-day money-back window for first-time subscribers).
I'm new to options. Is this platform suitable for me?
The Weekly Option Selling strategies require an F&O-enabled brokerage account and a basic understanding of options (strikes, premiums, spreads). If you are new, we recommend starting with the Basic plan and reading the Glossary and Methodology section before placing any option trades. The equity portfolio requires no options knowledge.
